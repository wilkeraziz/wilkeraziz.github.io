Read [this](https://vene.ro/mletter.html) before you apply for a position to work with me. I agree with every point Vlad makes in that statement. 

Also, let me add a few practical things:
* Please do not use AI to draft, write, or polish your letter. 
* Applicants sometimes ask me if they can use AI for grammar and spelling. My answer remains the same: please don't use it. I am not a native speaker and I don't mind grammar and spelling mistakes. The point of the motivation letter is for me to learn something _about you_, and AI gets on the way of that.
* I am available to answer questions you may have about any position I advertise, and you should write to me if you have a question whose answer does not seem clear enough from the listing. That said, I do not appreciate emails that are intended to do no more than 'to establish a first contact' or 'to get me to notice an application'. When the time comes, I personally review all applications that are received through the UvA portal. 
* If the job ad does not ask you to list referees, please don't list them and please don't email me to let me know who is ready and willing to provide a reference letter for you. 


If I have any positions available at the moment, you will find them listed [here](https://probabll.github.io/jobs/).
